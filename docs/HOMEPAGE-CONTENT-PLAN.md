## 2. Inner Pages Strategy (Relieving Homepage Overhead)

The homepage intentionally avoids company history essays, team biographies, extensive FAQs, or repetitive diagnostic quizzes:
- **`/about`**: Houses the full company background, engineering philosophy, and authentic credentials for Co-Founder **Atul Suryawanshi** (*M.Sc. Digital Factory & Operational Excellence*, HDBW Munich; *B.E. Mechanical Engineering*, SPPU Pune).
- **`/services/*`**: Dedicated deep-dives for each pillar with full technical capability guides, delivery phases, and targeted FAQs.
- **`/work` & `/work/[slug]`**: Comprehensive project case-study archive with architecture specs and client engagement details.
- **`/contact`**: Interactive project inquiry form with live Zod validation and direct scheduling options.
